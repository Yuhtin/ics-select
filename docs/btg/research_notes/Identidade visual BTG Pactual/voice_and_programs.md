# BTG Pactual: brand voice, early-career/tech programs, and Inteli relationship

Research date: 2026-09-29. Several BTG career pages (carreiras.btgpactual.com, ri.btgpactual.com/en partnership page, individual job posts) render client-side and returned no body text to the fetcher, and Glassdoor returned 403. Where that happened, a mirror (a job PDF, press, or an aggregator) is cited instead, and this is noted.

## 1. Tone of voice, taglines, and how BTG talks about meritocracy, partnership, performance, and excellence

### Takeaway
BTG's voice is ambitious, confident, and built around excellence. It is organized around a few recurring words: **excelência, inconformismo, meritocracia / "Partnership Meritocrático", espírito empreendedor / dono (ownership), longo prazo, cliente**, and "Maior Banco de Investimentos da América Latina". Recent consumer campaigns turn excellence into something the individual owns ("A excelência está em você") and use the verb-hashtag **#DêUmBTG** ("give it a BTG"), which also shows up in recruiting copy.

### Cited Findings
- Institutional site, "Quem somos": purpose "apoiar seu momento de vida e a construção da sua história"; motto "Superar expectativas é o que nos motiva. Existimos para superar o possível."; DNA "Busca pela excelência, espírito empreendedor, constante inconformismo e crescimento sustentável"; culture described as "Partnership Meritocrático" combining agility, client focus, excellence, autonomy and long-term vision. The page also stresses an ownership mentality, putting itself in the client's shoes, and a non-bureaucratic, flat model — [BTG Pactual, Quem somos](https://institucional.btgpactual.com/quem-somos)
- Stated values: integridade, responsabilidade, simplicidade, inconformismo, dedicação (plus sustainability/ESG) — [BTG Pactual, Quem somos](https://institucional.btgpactual.com/quem-somos)
- English phrasing (IR/Built In): "Since inception, in 1983, the Bank has been run based on a meritocratic partnership culture, focused on clients, excellence and a long-term vision"; "BTG Pactual operates as a Partnership, in which the Partners are also executives of the Bank." Note the capitalized "Bank", "Partnership", "Partners" — [Built In, BTG Pactual](https://builtin.com/company/btg-pactual); [BTG RI, Partnership](https://ri.btgpactual.com/en/sobre-o-banco/partnership) (page body did not render for the fetcher; text seen in search snippet)
- History: Pactual was known for a meritocracy policy where top employees were invited to become partners, and BTG grew on a culture of "partnership, meritocracia e ambição" — [Eu Quero Investir, história do BTG](https://euqueroinvestir.com/educacao-financeira/de-volta-ao-jogo-a-trajetoria-do-btg-pactual-contada-em-livro)
- Jan 2024 campaign **#DêumBTG**, agency Artplan: slogan "A excelência está em você". Excellence is framed as "dedicação, trabalho duro, inconformismo, atenção aos detalhes e paixão", which BTG aims to "potencializar ... em cada indivíduo". The films feature Alex Atala, Speto, Lethicia Bronstein and Instituto Baccarelli — [ABRAMARK](https://abramark.com.br/destaques/btg-pactual-destaca-a-forca-da-excelencia-em-nova-campanha-deumbtg/); [Exame](https://exame.com/marketing/btg-pactual-traz-alex-atala-speto-lethicia-bronstein-e-instituto-baccarelli-em-nova-campanha/)
- Jan 27, 2025 campaign (Artplan, ambassador Rodrigo Hilbert), positioning BTG as a "banco completo": slogan "Quem espera mais de um Banco, merece o BTG" (note the capital "Banco") — [Exame](https://exame.com/marketing/btg-pactual-lanca-campanha-com-rodrigo-hilbert-e-reforca-posicionamento-como-banco-completo/); [Meio & Mensagem](https://www.meioemensagem.com.br/comunicacao/btg-pactual-campanha)
- A related campaign line is "Dê um BTG nos seus sonhos" — [Grandes Nomes da Propaganda](https://grandesnomesdapropaganda.com.br/anunciantes/btg-pactual-apresneta-nova-campanha-de-um-btg-nos-seus-sonhos/) (not fetched; title only)
- Recruiting voice (Estágio Tech posting): "#DêUmBTG na sua carreira e faça parte da geração do Maior Banco de Investimentos da América Latina!"; "Venha se desenvolver com autonomia, crescer e fazer parte da transformação do mercado financeiro conosco."; "Por aqui, a 'mão na massa' e a meritocracia acontecem desde o primeiro dia!" — [Geração BTG Estágio Tech PDF, via IC Unicamp](https://ic.unicamp.br/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/4-Geracao-BTG-Estagio-Tech.pdf); the same intro text appears on [Greenhouse, Estágio de Férias board](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/btgpactualestagioferias)
- Estágio de Férias landing page headline: "Venha se desenvolver com autonomia e participar da transformação do mercado financeiro" — [BTG, Estágio de Férias](https://conteudo.btgpactual.com/estagio-de-ferias)
- Commentary on partnership models (not BTG-authored) notes that "up or out" and calibration rituals often go with meritocratic partnerships — [Merithu](https://merithu.com.br/2021/08/04/modelo-de-partnership-a-gestao-voltada-para-o-crescimento/)

### Inferences
- The words that will land in a pitch are the ones BTG already uses: *excelência, meritocracia, mão na massa desde o primeiro dia, autonomia, inconformismo, geração, dono/ownership, longo prazo*. An interview-prep platform lines up naturally with "A excelência está em você": excellence as a personal attribute you develop through work.
- "#DêUmBTG" is a campaign mechanic BTG owns. A co-branded product could plausibly echo it (for example, "Dê um BTG na sua preparação"), but it is a brand asset and needs BTG approval.
- Voice is confident and superlative ("Maior Banco de Investimentos da América Latina", "superar o possível") but not slangy. Avoid hype words that BTG itself doesn't use.

### Gaps
- No public brand-voice or style guide was found.
- The English careers site did not render, so there are no verbatim English careers taglines beyond the IR/Built In phrasing.
- Number of partners: not retrieved (the IR page body was blank).

## 2. Early-career and tech hiring programs, selection stages, skills emphasized

### Takeaway
BTG recruits tech talent mainly through **Estágio de Férias** (summer internship, Jan–Feb, for 1st–2nd-year students), **Estágio Regular** (1–2 years, later-year students), the "**Geração BTG**" internship umbrella, and targeted tracks: **Ela Faz Tech** (women developers), **Integra** (PwD interns), and historically a **Technology Program**. Selection runs through online tests (logic/English/technical, "SkillCert"), group dynamics or panels, and interviews with managers and partners. Skills that recur: logical reasoning, analytical ability, OOP, backend (C#/.NET, Java, Python), APIs/microservices, cloud, and an entrepreneurial, high-energy attitude.

### Cited Findings
- Entry-programs hub: [BTG Carreiras, Programas de Entrada](https://carreiras.btgpactual.com/programas-de-entrada); tech hub: [Oportunidades Tech](https://carreiras.btgpactual.com/tecnologia) (both render client-side; content not retrieved)
- Estágio de Férias 2027: for students in the first two years of Technology, Engineering, Business or related courses, in person in SP or RJ, starting Jan 4, 2027. Stages: applications (20/07–21/08), **SkillCert** tests (20/07–25/08), online assessment panels (01/09–25/09), final feedback by 23/10 — [BTG, Estágio de Férias](https://conteudo.btgpactual.com/estagio-de-ferias)
- The summer intern builds "a short-term project aimed at optimizing area workflows" alongside daily routines, with the chance to lead projects and grow "based on merit" — [Estágio Trainee](https://www.estagiotrainee.com/post/programa-estagio-ferias-btg-pactual-2027)
- Geração BTG Estágio Tech (2023/24): the intern receives a tech project "com início, meio e fim" to present to the area, for internships from 1 month to 2 years. "Esperamos de você": raciocínio lógico e capacidade analítica; proatividade e energia; curiosidade e paixão em aprender novas habilidades e linguagens; adaptabilidade e resiliência. Engenharia da Computação / Sistemas de Informação preferred; OOP knowledge a plus; stipend R$ 2.600 — [Geração BTG Estágio Tech PDF](https://ic.unicamp.br/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/4-Geracao-BTG-Estagio-Tech.pdf)
- Backend developer intern (BTG Empresas): APIs and microservices, scalable/resilient applications with C#, .NET Core and distributed architectures; 4th semester onward; interest in cloud, databases, messaging and software-engineering best practices — [BTG Carreiras job post](https://carreiras.btgpactual.com/vagas/btg-empresas/estagio-desenvolvedor-backend/6001488004) (via search snippet; the page did not render)
- 2026: technology is described as the largest source of BTG openings (31%), including AI analysts (Salesforce), AI consultants for customer service, and retail-banking digital-transformation developers. Stacks cited: Python, Java, .NET, ReactJS, automation, APIs. The typical process is CV screening, logic and English tests, group dynamics, and interviews with area managers — [Gran Faculdade blog](https://faculdade.grancursosonline.com.br/blog/estagio-btg-pactual-2026/) (aggregator; the 31% figure is unverified against a primary source); see also [Na Prática, June 2026](https://napratica.org.br/noticias/btg-pactual-itau-ambev-as-melhores-vagas-de-estagio-de-junho-de-2026)
- Technology Program (2018/19 edition): technical exam → logic test → in-person dynamics with managers (SP/RJ) → **collective interview with partners**. Wanted: CS/IS/Computer/Electrical engineering, fluent English desirable, "espírito empreendedor" — [Na Prática](https://www.napratica.org.br/btg-pactual-technology-program/)
- Ela Faz Tech (2024): 14 analyst positions for women back-end developers who graduated 2022–2023, with mentorship from female leaders and two rotations in tech areas. Integra: internship for people with disabilities — [Exame](https://exame.com/carreira/btg-pactual-oferece-programa-para-mulheres-na-tecnologia-e-estagio-para-pessoas-com-deficiencia/)
- Glassdoor reports summarized in search results: tests/video, group stage, several individual interviews with managers assessing potential, cultural fit and technical knowledge; average process about 49 days; overall rating 3.7/5 — [Glassdoor Interviews](https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/BTG-Pactual-Interview-Questions-E411540.htm) (403 on fetch; snippet only, treat as low confidence)
- #BTGFazTech Hackathon (Open Finance): 700+ registrations, 120 pre-selected, prep with AWS, 21 finalist teams, prizes over R$ 80k — [Exame](https://exame.com/negocios/btg-pactual-realiza-hackathon-com-foco-em-open-finance/) (via search snippet)

### Inferences
- A prep platform maps directly onto BTG's funnel: logic/raciocínio tests, SkillCert/technical tests, painéis/dinâmicas, and partner interviews (fit, ownership). "Preparation for the whole funnel" is a stronger pitch than "LeetCode for BTG".
- BTG's stack signals (C#/.NET, Java, Python, React, APIs, cloud, AI) suggest the library content a BTG-branded version should put first.
- Partner interviews are the part that is distinctly BTG. Behavioral/fit prep around *meritocracia, dono, inconformismo* is a differentiator.

### Gaps
- No verified "BTG Tech" branded trainee program for 2026. A distinct quant track or its test content wasn't found.
- What SkillCert assesses exactly (coding vs. aptitude) was not confirmed.
- Glassdoor details (specific coding questions) couldn't be fetched.

## 3. BTG partnerships with universities, Inteli, education initiatives, hackathons

### Takeaway
Inteli (Instituto de Tecnologia e Liderança, São Paulo) was **created by André Esteves, Roberto Sallouti and BTG Pactual partners**, funded by a **R$ 200 million donation from the Esteves family with institutional support from BTG Pactual**. It opened in February 2022 as Brazil's first project-based computing college. Sallouti (BTG CEO) chairs Inteli's board, and BTG hosts Inteli student projects and interns. Legally it is a non-profit separate from the bank. It was founded by BTG's partners, not by the bank itself.

### Cited Findings
- Founding, donors and board: Esteves family R$ 200M donation plus BTG institutional support; created by André Esteves, Roberto Sallouti and BTG partners; conceived in 2019, first classes Feb 2022. Board: Roberto Sallouti (president); André and Lilian Esteves (sponsors); members include Mark Maletz and Silvio Meira; CEO Maíra Habimorad — [Capital Digital](https://capitaldigital.com.br/socios-do-btg-pactual-criam-o-inteli-instituto-de-tecnologia-e-lideranca/); [Empreendedor](https://empreendedor.com.br/tecnologia/socios-do-btg-pactual-criam-instituto-de-tecnologia-e-lideranca/); [Exame](https://exame.com/carreira/socios-do-btg-lancam-instituto-de-tecnologia-e-lideranca-o-inteli/)
- Framed as a non-profit inspired by MIT/Stanford, press-dubbed the "MIT brasileiro" — [SindInfor](https://sindinfor.org.br/esteves-do-btg-doa-r-200-milhoes-para-criar-faculdade-de-tecnologia-e-computacao/); [Diário do Rio](https://diariodorio.com/btg-pactual-anuncia-criacao-do-mit-brasileiro/)
- Esteves quote: Inteli "nasceu da necessidade do país de se tornar mais relevante no desenvolvimento de tecnologia"; Sallouti: the mission is forming leaders "who will help transform Brazil" — [Capital Digital](https://capitaldigital.com.br/socios-do-btg-pactual-criam-o-inteli-instituto-de-tecnologia-e-lideranca/) (quotes translated by the fetch tool; wording may differ from the original)
- As of Jul 2024: more than half of students on scholarship (about R$ 119k/yr per student, covering tuition, transport, English and a computer); students from 95 cities; interns placed at Uber, Amazon, iFood and **BTG**; Sallouti joins monthly presentations; BTG's BoostLab involved in student entrepreneurship. Sallouti: "Nossa meta é sermos reconhecidos como uma das melhores faculdades de computação da América Latina" — [IstoÉ Dinheiro](https://istoedinheiro.com.br/faculdade-criada-por-socios-do-btg-ja-tem-startups-e-lancara-pos-em-ciberseguranca)
- Inteli's module model: each module is a real project for a market partner. First-year students built games for onboarding in BTG's IT Funds department — [Brasil 247](https://www.brasil247.com/negocios/inteli-aposta-em-formacao-de-talentos-para-reduzir-deficit-em-tecnologia/) (via search snippet)
- Inteli launched a cybersecurity postgraduate program — [Bloomberg Línea](https://www.bloomberglinea.com.br/negocios/inteli-de-socios-do-btg-entra-em-pos-graduacao-com-novo-curso-de-ciberseguranca/)
- Other BTG university touchpoints: the Geração BTG Estágio Tech posting was distributed through Unicamp's IC — [IC Unicamp PDF](https://ic.unicamp.br/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/4-Geracao-BTG-Estagio-Tech.pdf); a university blockchain hackathon with R$ 75k in prizes (Exame Future of Money) — [Exame](https://exame.com/future-of-money/projetos-de-universitarios-com-blockchain-sao-premiados-em-r-75-mil-durante-hackathon/) (BTG's role there was not verified)

### Inferences
- The pitch can truthfully say "built by students at Inteli, the college founded by BTG's partners". Avoid saying "BTG's college" or implying the bank owns Inteli.
- There is precedent for Inteli students delivering real projects to BTG (the IT Funds onboarding games), which makes an ICS Select pilot look like a natural next "Inteli × BTG" project.
- BTG already runs a hackathon brand (#BTGFazTech) and diversity tracks (Ela Faz Tech, Integra). The platform could be offered as prep infrastructure for those cohorts.

### Gaps
- No evidence was found of a formal Inteli → BTG hiring pipeline or of BTG-specific recruiting quotas at Inteli.
- How involved Inteli Consulting Society (the student club) is with BTG: not found.
- Current (2026) Inteli student numbers were not verified.

## 4. Language conventions (pt-BR vs. English, você, capitalization)

### Takeaway
Member-facing and careers copy is **pt-BR, addressing the reader as "você"**, and mixes in finance/tech English freely ("Partnership Meritocrático", "feedback", "SkillCert", plus hashtags like #DêUmBTG, #BTGFazTech), next to colloquial pt-BR such as "mão na massa". BTG capitalizes institutional nouns heavily: "Maior Banco de Investimentos da América Latina", "Banco", "Partnership", "Partners", "Estágio de Férias", "Estágio Regular", "Bolsa Auxílio".

### Cited Findings
- "Você receberá um projeto...", "Esperamos de você:" — direct "você" address throughout — [Geração BTG Estágio Tech PDF](https://ic.unicamp.br/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/4-Geracao-BTG-Estagio-Tech.pdf)
- Title-cased superlative "Maior Banco de Investimentos da América Latina" in recruiting copy — [Greenhouse board](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/btgpactualestagioferias)
- "Quem espera mais de um Banco, merece o BTG" capitalizes "Banco" mid-sentence — [Exame](https://exame.com/marketing/btg-pactual-lanca-campanha-com-rodrigo-hilbert-e-reforca-posicionamento-como-banco-completo/)
- The hashtag is written in camel-case "#DêUmBTG" (careers) and "#DêumBTG" (ABRAMARK's rendering of the campaign) — [Greenhouse board](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/btgpactualestagioferias); [ABRAMARK](https://abramark.com.br/destaques/btg-pactual-destaca-a-forca-da-excelencia-em-nova-campanha-deumbtg/)
- The Estágio de Férias page uses "você" with English loans such as "feedback" and "SkillCert" — [BTG, Estágio de Férias](https://conteudo.btgpactual.com/estagio-de-ferias)
- "Partnership Meritocrático" is an English noun with a Portuguese adjective on the institutional site — [BTG, Quem somos](https://institucional.btgpactual.com/quem-somos)
- Business lines are named in English: Investment Banking, Corporate Lending, Sales & Trading, Wealth Management, Asset Management — [Built In](https://builtin.com/company/btg-pactual)

### Inferences
- For a BTG-facing product, member-facing copy in pt-BR with "você" and English kept for industry terms fits BTG's own practice. This roughly matches ICS Select's current convention (English UI chrome, pt-BR content), but BTG leans more pt-BR in headings. Consider pt-BR headings with English technical nouns.
- Match BTG's capitalization of the institution ("o Banco", "Partners") when referring to BTG, and keep the platform's own chrome consistent.

### Gaps
- The BTG app/banking UI copy (e.g., whether nav labels are in English) was not examined; no source was found describing BTG digital product microcopy conventions.
